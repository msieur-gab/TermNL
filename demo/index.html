What this is

TermNL lets you talk to a Linux board in plain words, for people who aren't comfortable with the terminal. It asks for whatever is missing, asks for approval when an action is risky, and only ever runs commands written in its skill files: small text files anyone can write.

This is TermNL itself, running in this page on a pretend board. Commands are shown, never run. Every request ends in one of four outcomes: act ask approve abstain.

Inbox

    What TermNL doesn't understand lands here, and so does a guess you confirm with a yes: on the board, that is what you teach it next.

    Teach it a skill

    A skill is a text file: what people say, the command it runs, what it replies. Change this one, or write your own.

    TermNL knows the sentences under ## People say at once. The ## Also said lines teach its small model other ways of saying them: say “learn what I taught you” and it trains again, here in your browser, in a few minutes, while you keep talking. What you teach stays in this tab.
