Reads the skill files again, as they are now (what people say, Also said, their words), on this device, in
the background: well under a second, even on an Orange Pi Zero 2W. Nothing is trained (ALIGN.md, 25 Oct 2026):
the reader counts the skills' words when they load. A skill file with a problem leaves what was known before
in place, and the reply says what is wrong. The learning inbox (what a "yes" taught, reviewed by the person)
comes next.
