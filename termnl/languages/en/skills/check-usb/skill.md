## People say

- is (my/the/a/an) usb plugged in

- check (the/my) usb
- what is plugged/connected (in/into/to) (the) (usb/board)
- what's plugged/connected (in/into/to) (the) (usb/board)
- is (my/the/a) (meshtastic/lora/usb/external/hard) radio/node/headset/speaker/drive/stick/device/disk plugged/connected (in)
- do/did/can you see (my/the/a) (meshtastic/lora/usb/external/hard) radio/node/headset/speaker/drive/stick/device/disk

Gab, 24 Nov: "if that's a mesh network or a node, a headset, a speaker, that makes a bigger difference. Or a hard drive
or a USB stick, you know, it makes a difference." Each thing on the USB ports by its own name and what kind it is: a
drive and its size, sound, a Meshtastic radio… A board's own chips on USB can show too (the Lyra's Wi-Fi): the device
can't tell them apart.
