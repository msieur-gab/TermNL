## Opposites

An action said in so many words is never answered with its opposite: "turn the board on" doesn't offer to
shut it down. Each line: an action, then its opposites.

- turn on: turn off, shut down
- turn off: turn on
- shut down: turn on
- play: stop, pause
- mute: unmute
- unmute: mute
- open: close
- close: open
- next: previous
- previous: next
- turn up: turn down, mute
- turn down: turn up

## Meaning with an object

Moved into the skills (25 Oct 2026): "turn on the music" plays it because play-music says so, under its own
`## Words` (`- turn on, switch on, start → play`). A skill dropped in brings what its words mean; this list
stays for a language that hasn't moved them yet (read into the skills of that action and those objects).
